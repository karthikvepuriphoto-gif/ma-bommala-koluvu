# Music licenses

The four built-in film tracks were synthesized for this app. They are not recordings, samples, or arrangements of existing songs. A small Python script (`scripts/make_music.py`) builds every sound from sine waves, a short plucked-string model, and shaped noise. Because the audio is original and generated here, it is not expected to match a YouTube Content ID reference.

Each file is about 12 seconds, mono, and meant to loop. They are stored as small MP3s in `public/music/`.

| `flute.mp3` | Slow original pentatonic flute line over a quiet drone | `scripts/make_music.py` (`flute`) | [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) |

| `drums.mp3` | Light original drum pattern with a few flute notes | `scripts/make_music.py` (`drums`) | [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) |

You may copy, change, and share these four files for any purpose, including a family film. No attribution is required.

Music someone picks with **Use my own music** stays on their phone and in their film. That file is theirs. This app does not grant any license for it, and a personal recording can still be flagged by YouTube if it contains someone else's song.
